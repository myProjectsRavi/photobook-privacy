## Full Description (max 4000 chars) — Character count: 2847

PhotoBook is the photo search app that respects your privacy. Search your entire photo library using natural language — completely offline, with zero data leaving your device.

Think of it as a phone book, but for your photos.

■ WHY PHOTOBOOK?

Every other photo app wants to upload your memories to the cloud. PhotoBook does the opposite. It runs entirely on your device — no internet connection required, no accounts, no sign-ups, no cloud sync. Your photos stay yours.

■ SEARCH NATURALLY

Just type what you're looking for:
• "selfie" — finds your selfies
• "food" — finds food photos
• "Hyderabad" — finds photos taken in Hyderabad
• "last week" — finds photos from the past 7 days
• "today camera" — finds today's camera shots
• "document" — finds screenshots, receipts, and text-heavy images
• "yesterday sunset" — finds sunset photos from yesterday
• "favorites" — shows your starred photos

Combine any keywords. Search by date, location, folder, content type, or text inside your photos.

■ SMART ON-DEVICE AI

PhotoBook uses Google ML Kit to understand your photos — all processing happens on your device:

• Image Labeling: Automatically tags photos as food, nature, pets, cars, people, buildings, and more
• Face Detection: Identifies selfies and group photos
• OCR (Text Recognition): Finds text inside your photos — search for receipts, documents, screenshots by the text they contain
• Offline Geocoding: Converts GPS coordinates to searchable city and country names without any network call

■ LIGHTNING-FAST SEARCH

Powered by SQLite FTS4 (Full-Text Search), PhotoBook delivers instant results across thousands of photos. Incremental sync means only new or changed photos are re-indexed — keeping battery usage minimal.

■ FEATURES AT A GLANCE

• 100% offline — zero network permissions, zero internet access
• Natural language search — type dates, places, or content types
• On-device AI tagging — selfie, food, pet, document, nature, and more
• OCR search — find text inside photos
• Location search — search by city, state, or country
• Folder-aware — search camera, screenshots, downloads, WhatsApp
• Favorites — star and filter your best photos
• Multi-select and share — select multiple photos and share instantly
• Full-screen viewer — swipe through search results
• Smart suggestions — auto-complete as you type
• Incremental sync — battery-efficient, only processes changes
• Dark mode — follows your system theme
• Material 3 design — clean, modern, distraction-free

■ PRIVACY GUARANTEE

PhotoBook requests only two permissions: photo access and media location (for GPS-based search). It does NOT request internet or network permissions. There are no analytics, no crash reporters, no ad trackers, no telemetry. Nothing is uploaded. Ever.

Your photos are searched, tagged, and stored in a private on-device database that is automatically deleted when you uninstall the app.

■ BUILT FOR EVERYONE

Whether you have 500 photos or 50,000, PhotoBook handles your library efficiently. Works on Android 8.0 and above.

Stop letting cloud services scan your memories. Take back control of your photo library.

PhotoBook — Your memories. Your privacy. Offline.
